Canada First Bricks Inc has been in operation since 2017, initially specializing in the distribution of LEGO pieces. We quickly expanded our inventory and are now one of the key players in the industry worldwide. We are present on the specialized platform ‘Bricklink,’ owned by ‘The Lego Group.’ This platform hosts more than 19,000 merchants, and for the past few years, we have been among the top 20 stores offering the highest number of pieces both in quantity and variety. Additionally, we are among the top 20 stores with the most positive transaction-related reviews. In 2022, we decided to also integrate the BrickOwl platform.

Since 2021, we have been assembling models for private distributors. We now have several years of experience and have produced nearly one hundred thousand units since the humble beginnings of our assembly division. We now have a team of 15 employees dedicated to advancing the company by helping our many clients develop their own businesses. We offer a turnkey or à la carte service, allowing you to outsource one or several stages of your product distribution. Our turnkey solution frees up your time to focus on developing and selling your models while we take care of everything else.

We also launched our own software in 2022 and have since added dozens of features, making it a very powerful and unique tool. This software enables us to offer you unparalleled service by allowing us to fully manage all the production stages of your models.

Here is a detailed list:

🌟 SUBMISSIONS

Our software allows us to quickly and easily provide you with the most competitive price for the model you want us to produce.

🌟 Assistance with Part Optimization

With our software, it is very easy for us to identify problematic parts and help you resolve the issue.

🌟 Customer Orders

Once your order is received, it is entered into our software, and you will receive an order confirmation, ensuring that nothing has been overlooked.

🌟 Purchasing

Our software allows us to process purchases in record time thanks to several automated steps. This software helps reduce costs for you and ultimately allows you to have a better margin on your sales or offer better prices to your customers.
We have facilities in the United States and Lithuania, enabling us to acquire parts efficiently, effectively, and quickly.

🌟 Parts Inventory

We have access to over 8 million pieces in stock, allowing us to shorten production times for your models. This means less waiting time for out-of-stock models.

🌟 Additional Item Inventory Management

With the help of our software, we can easily manage the inventory of non-LEGO parts. Here are some examples:

  • Printed parts: We offer external services for vinyl sticker printing, UV printing on pieces, and PAD-type printing. It is even possible to print on assembled parts. You provide the design, and they handle the printing.

  • High-quality digital printing on-site: We have an in-house Konica Minolta C1070 digital press equipped with a Fiery system. This tool includes RIP software and allows us to print documents with vibrant colors by using Pantone color codes for an impressive result.
    We can print various page formats that can be added to your models, such as QR codes for digital instructions, stapled instruction booklets up to 40 pages, and glued booklets up to 250 pages.

  • Lighting kits: Want to allow your customers to add a unique touch to their models? We are now distributors of ‘Light My Bricks’ products, the reference for LEGO brick-based lighting, with products that integrate seamlessly into your models.

  • Printed packaging: Want to make the unboxing experience of your models unique? We work with a box manufacturer who can print your design on your packaging. For smaller models, we also collaborate with a premium flexible packaging manufacturer (large volume required).

🌟 Order Tracking, Storage, and Production Planning

Our software allows us to easily track placed orders, available inventory, and manage the production of your models through several automated processes.

🌟 Assembly

We employ a mechanical engineer with experience in production engineering. We now assemble the necessary parts for your models using state-of-the-art production machines. This includes an AI-powered part detection machine that ensures each piece in the assembly is correct. This machine brings precision to perfection, as we strive for 100% production quality.
Our production machine allows us to package your models according to your specifications. The required pieces for your model are sealed in numbered bags that can be customized by you, helping your customer follow the construction steps. Each bag can contain up to 30 different pieces.
We now have a very high production capacity, helping to reduce lead times. Most of our productions are completed in under 5 weeks, meaning you no longer need to order your models months in advance.

🌟 Completed Model Inventory Management and Outsourced Logistics

Our software allows you to easily check the quantity of your models ready for your customers, those pending production, and those already shipped. You can confidently plan your next purchases since all the necessary information is in one place.
We have negotiated very advantageous contracts with Canada Post, DHL, and other carriers, allowing us to ship your sales quickly at highly competitive rates. We have also integrated Shopify and Wix into our software, enabling us to retrieve, prepare, and fulfill your orders instantly. Tracking information is updated via their APIs, ensuring accuracy.
We can easily integrate other sales platforms since we have our own full-time programmer.

🌟 Communications

We have a dedicated employee with years of experience in client communications. They provide all the necessary information to help you offer top-notch customer service and assist with order tracking, ensuring each customer purchase is a memorable experience.

🌟 Customer Access to Our Software

With your own access to our software, you can easily track the production progress of your sets, follow your orders, and monitor your customers’ orders. You have all the tools needed to grow your business.

Started from humble beginnings in 2017, our goal has always been to become one of the top distributors for Lego parts in the whole world.
